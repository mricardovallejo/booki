What is BooKI

A reading companion
that knows your page.

Open a PDF — a textbook, a paper, a novel, your own notes — and read at your own pace. Whenever you want, ask BooKI a question by text or voice. It answers using the pages around where you are.

  • Ask anything about the page you're on, and get an answer grounded in what you're actually reading.
  • Quiz yourself on the pages you've already covered — answers are marked with a score and specific feedback.
  • Get summaries and plain-language explanations without ever leaving the reader.
  • Nothing is gated. BooKI travels along with you; it doesn't turn the book into homework.

1
Step one

Add a book.

On the home screen, tap Upload PDF and pick any PDF. It joins your library, sorted into Recently added, your own tags, and Long reads. Search by title to jump back in later.

Step two

Start a session.

BooKIStart a session
3
Step three

Read, and ask.

The PDF is on the left, the conversation on the right. Move through the pages as you like — BooKI records the furthest one you reach.

  • Ask about "this page" and BooKI answers from the pages around you.
  • Name a page range and it uses that instead.
  • Use the quick actions — Ask me, Explain, Summarize, Memorize — for one-tap requests.
BooKIRead, and ask
Text or voice

Talk to it, or type.

Tap the microphone and speak your question. BooKI transcribes it, answers in the same conversation, and can read the answer back aloud — or keep it text-only with the speaker toggle. Voice and text share one history: it's the same conversation either way.

BooKIText or voice
4
Step four

Check yourself.

On the Quiz tab, pick a page range you've read and BooKI writes a short quiz. Answer in your own words — each answer comes back with a percentage score and a specific note on what to add, not just right or wrong.

BooKICheck yourself
Summaries & explanations

Catch up in a
paragraph.

Ask for a summary of a range of pages — it combines the book's text with your discussion so far. The result lands in the chat, or you can download it as a PDF. Tap Explain on a passage you didn't follow and BooKI unpacks it in plain language, right where you are.

BooKISummaries
Make it yours

Tune how it teaches.
Track how it's going.

In Reading setup, a tutor profile is the full set of instructions a session runs on — its persona, what each difficulty level means, and how it handles quizzes, summaries and explanations. A reader profile describes who's reading — their level, goal and subject. Keep a different one per subject you study.

Every session also has a Progress tab: pages read, questions asked, quiz scores, and a report you can email or download.
